==================================================

in the dedicated "rosec demo" vault. They were read through the actual rosec CLI,
private D-Bus, rosecd, Extism WASM guest and live Proton API.

pointed to a temporary private bus. That daemon has now stopped and its PAT has
been revoked: these IDs are not currently in the user's regular rosec instance.
