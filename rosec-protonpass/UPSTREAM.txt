Distribution
============
This spike is not included in rosec release packaging. A distributed binary must
be accompanied by GPL-compliant corresponding source/build materials and applicable
third-party notices. Cargo.lock records the dependency versions. Host/plugin combined
distribution review remains required; WASM alone does not establish a licence boundary.
